Controller setup RealFlight Evolution

Channels
Throttle, aileron, elevator, rudder, plus flaps on switch D
Flaps
Switch D: up / mid / down = 0 / 15 / 30°
Aileron
15° up / 12° down (differential is in the model)
Elevator
12° each way
Rudder
30° each way
Expo (suggested)
About 30% on aileron and elevator to start, tune to taste
Power on
Thrust line sits ≈ 105 mm above the wing, so power pitches the nose down. Hold a little up elevator in the climb.

Throttle: the lift opens above 8%. Keep the stick fully down while gliding or the lid pops open. If you use a throttle cut, set it below 8%.
